The brand lockup — use `lockup` in headers and marketing, `mark` alone at ≤24px or in favicons/app icons.

```jsx
<Logo size={32} />
<Logo variant="mark" size={24} />
```

Never recolour "Knight"; never set the wordmark in anything but Space Grotesk Bold. Minimum clear space around the lockup is the width of the shield mark. Raw files: `assets/logo.svg`, `assets/mark.svg`, `assets/wordmark.svg`.
